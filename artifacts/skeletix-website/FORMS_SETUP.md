# Contact and inquiry delivery

The Contact page no longer embeds or links to the published Google Form. It keeps Skeletix's email address and explains that visitors can write about SkeletID, investment, partnerships, or collaboration.

Selecting the email link opens the visitor's configured email application with Skeletix's address. The website does not collect contact submissions or promise an automatic reply. If the visitor has no email application configured, they can copy the address and contact Skeletix another way.

This website change does not delete or modify the separate Google Form or its existing responses in Google. It is no longer linked from the website. Changes to the form or its records must be made directly in Google Forms.

## Investor inquiries

The separate custom Investor inquiry form remains disabled. Visitors can use the email address on the Contact page. The disabled Formspree configuration is kept separate from Contact and must not be enabled without its own setup decision.

No passwords or private credentials belong in frontend code.
