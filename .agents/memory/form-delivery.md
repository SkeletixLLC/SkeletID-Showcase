name: Simple inquiry delivery
description: User's Google Forms choice for the Contact page and simplicity requirement.
---

Keep the Contact page simple: show the listed company email and briefly identify what people can email about. Do not embed the old Google Form on the website.

**Why:** The user first supplied a Google Form for Contact, then decided the existing email address and a short inquiry description would be simpler.

**How to apply:** Keep Contact email-only unless the user asks to restore a form. Email links open the visitor's email application; the site does not deliver or automatically notify on submissions. Keep the separate Investor form's delivery decision distinct from the user's Contact-page choice.

Removing the form from the website does not delete the form or responses stored in the owner's Google account.

**Why:** The Contact-page email-only change only removes the embed and link. The form and its records remain under Google account control.

**How to apply:** Do not claim external Google Form data was deleted or changed by website edits.
